# Product Identity and Differentiation

This document defines the canonical product identity and differentiation doctrine for the ASO-X repository.

## Identity

ASO-X is intended to be a durable, governance-aware, strategically managed platform with strong artifact retention, intelligence value, continuity discipline, and extensibility.

## Differentiation Principles

- Preserve a unique control-plane-driven operating model.
- Treat governance, retention, and strategic continuity as first-class product assets.
- Maintain a foundation suitable for derivative and downstream project adaptation.
- Favor real operational utility over superficial novelty.
- Preserve coherence between enterprise-grade rigor, autonomous operation, and long-term commercial value.

## Uniqueness Requirement

The project should remain distinct, extensible, and professionally defensible rather than becoming a shallow wrapper, fragmented experiment, or memory-dependent workflow.
