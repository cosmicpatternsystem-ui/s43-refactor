# Canonical Roadmap

This document is the canonical roadmap for the ASO-X repository.

Any other roadmap must either align with this document or be treated as non-authoritative.

## Horizon 0  Repository Stability

- Keep main clean, synchronized, and validation-covered.
- Ensure canonical entrypoints remain discoverable.

## Horizon 1  Governance Enforcement

- Maintain release-blocking governance contracts.
- Prevent drift between README, governance index, manifest, and critical governance documents.

## Horizon 2  Strategic Continuity

- Enforce a canonical strategic control plane.
- Preserve objectives, roadmap, monetization doctrine, durability doctrine, and continuity guidance as repository-native artifacts.

## Horizon 3  Automation and Self-Healing

- Expand validation to detect contradictions, drift, omissions, and stale strategic artifacts.
- Prefer deterministic repair paths and safe automation.

## Horizon 4  Productization

- Define reusable product surfaces, interfaces, and extension patterns.
- Preserve adaptability for multiple deployment profiles.

## Horizon 5  Commercialization

- Prioritize work that increases monetization capacity, defensibility, and operating leverage.
- Reduce manual overhead and increase value density.

## Horizon 6  Enterprise Deployment

- Strengthen reliability, auditability, change safety, and operational trust.
- Preserve governance suitable for institutional use.

## Horizon 7  Ecosystem and Extensibility

- Support derivative projects through a stable core and adaptable overlays.
- Maintain clarity between canonical core and customizable extensions.

## Horizon 8  Long-Term Durability

- Reduce dependency decay risk.
- Preserve archival readability and migration readiness.

## Horizon 9  50-Year Survival and Evolution

- Keep the project operationally legible, strategically coherent, and technologically adaptable across decades.
- Preserve the ability for new operators or agents to continue the correct professional path directly from the repository.
