# Commercial Controls Enforcement Pack

Status: Canonical  
Type: Normative  
Governance: Repository-governed  
Enforcement: Commercially binding  

## Purpose

This document defines the enforcement surface for commercial decision-making within the repository-governed operating model. It converts commercial doctrine and authority allocation into auditable, testable, and operationally enforceable controls.

This pack exists to ensure that commercial decisions affecting pricing, revenue quality, payment certainty, delivery commitments, counterparty exposure, and contractual deviations are not merely described, but controlled.

## Scope

This enforcement pack applies to all repository-governed commercial actions, including but not limited to:

- pricing decisions
- discounting
- non-standard commercial terms
- payment term deviations
- implementation and delivery promises
- multi-year commitments
- legal-commercial concessions
- exception handling
- renewal decisions
- partner and channel commercial structures

## Enforcement Principles

The following principles are mandatory:

1. No commercial exception is valid if it is not documented.
2. No commercial approval is valid if approver authority cannot be verified.
3. No commercial commitment is acceptable if delivery reality cannot support it.
4. No revenue position is acceptable if cash realization risk is ignored.
5. No margin concession is acceptable without explicit approval at the correct authority level.
6. No side agreement outside repository-governed controls is recognized as valid internally.

## Required Evidence

Every commercial exception, approval, or controlled deviation MUST have sufficient evidence to support review, approval, and retrospective audit.

Minimum evidence includes:

- counterparty identity
- commercial owner
- authority level invoked
- baseline commercial position
- requested deviation
- expected revenue impact
- expected margin impact
- expected cash impact
- delivery impact statement
- identified risk statement
- named approver
- approval timestamp
- immutable repository trace
- linked decision artifact

If the minimum evidence package is incomplete, the commercial action is incomplete.

## Hard Stop Conditions

The following conditions create an automatic block and MUST be treated as NO-GO until explicitly resolved through a repository-governed approval path:

- undocumented discount
- undocumented non-standard term
- margin below approved floor
- unapproved payment extension
- delivery promise not supported by execution reality
- missing approver authority
- counterparty risk unresolved
- side agreement outside canonical controls
- exception without expiry definition
- exception without risk statement
- approval without immutable traceability

Undocumented = invalid.  
Unapproved = non-binding.  
Unverifiable = rejected.  
Commercially unsafe = blocked.

## Exception Workflow

Every exception MUST follow a defined workflow:

1. Exception request is recorded.
2. Requested deviation is described precisely.
3. Justification is documented.
4. Risk statement is documented.
5. Margin, cash, delivery, and counterparty impacts are stated.
6. Approval route is mapped to authority level.
7. Approval or rejection is recorded.
8. Expiry date is defined where applicable.
9. Review cadence is defined for non-permanent exceptions.
10. Revocation condition is defined.

No exception may persist indefinitely without explicit renewal.

## Approval Integrity

Approval integrity requires all of the following:

- the approver exists
- the approver has the required authority
- the approval scope matches the requested deviation
- the approval is timestamped
- the approval is linked to immutable repository evidence
- the approval is attributable to a responsible owner

An approval artifact that fails any of the above is defective.

## Cash and Margin Protection

Commercial controls MUST preserve financial integrity, not merely support booking behavior.

Mandatory controls include:

- cash certainty outranks booked optimism
- revenue quality outranks headline revenue
- margin discipline outranks discretionary discounting
- payment extensions require explicit scrutiny
- concession stacking requires elevated review
- multi-year commercial positions require durability review
- collection risk must be considered before approval

## Delivery Truth Controls

Commercial commitments MUST remain bounded by delivery truth.

The following are prohibited unless explicitly reviewed and approved under controlled exception:

- promising unsupported functionality
- promising unsupported timelines
- promising unsupported operating guarantees
- promising unsupported integration certainty
- committing delivery outcomes without execution validation

Delivery truth outranks sales enthusiasm.

## Counterparty Fitness Controls

Commercial progression MUST stop when counterparty fitness is materially unresolved.

Counterparty blocking triggers include:

- unresolved identity concerns
- payment reliability concerns
- sanctions, compliance, or material integrity concerns
- structurally opaque buying authority
- persistent mismatch between commitment and capacity to perform

If counterparty fitness is unknown, the deal is not yet commercially safe.

## Auditability and Retention

All controlled commercial decisions MUST be auditable.

Minimum audit expectations:

- canonical artifact location
- durable naming
- traceability to commit or PR
- retention consistent with repository governance
- reproducible approval path
- evidence sufficient for independent review

If a decision cannot be reconstructed from retained evidence, the decision is governance-defective.

## Enforcement Posture

This repository adopts a hard enforcement posture for commercial controls.

The following interpretations are mandatory:

- undocumented commitments are invalid
- approvals outside authority are invalid
- repository-external side promises are unenforceable internally
- unverifiable exceptions are rejected
- unresolved hard-stop conditions block progression
- missing evidence blocks sign-off

## Minimum Review Triggers

Elevated review is required for any of the following:

- pricing below standard bounds
- discount stacking
- multi-year non-standard commitments
- payment terms outside approved norms
- exceptions affecting margin floor
- high-risk counterparties
- implementation promises with uncertain delivery basis
- renewals involving material concessions
- channel structures with unclear accountability

## Renewal and Revalidation

No commercial exception should automatically roll forward into renewal.

Renewals MUST re-evaluate:

- margin quality
- payment behavior
- cash realization
- delivery truth
- counterparty fitness
- concession history
- authority basis for continued deviation

Historical precedent alone is not approval.

## Repository Control Standard

Commercial control evidence MUST be repository-native wherever governance requires it.

This means:

- approval paths must be referenceable
- decision artifacts must be discoverable
- audit evidence must be retained
- control failures must be visible
- commercial ambiguity must not override control discipline

## Enforcement

This document is normative.

Repository-governed commercial decision flows, approval paths, and control checks MUST interpret this document as binding guidance for:

- commercial exceptions
- approval validity
- financial guardrail enforcement
- delivery commitment review
- counterparty gating
- audit and retention sufficiency

Any local practice that weakens these controls is subordinate to this document.
