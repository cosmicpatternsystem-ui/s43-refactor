# Global Commercial Hardening Addendum

## Purpose
This addendum codifies hardening controls for top-level commercial operation under real-money conditions, with bias toward irreversible correctness, controlled execution, and failure-intolerant decision framing.

## Core Commercial Hardening Principles
1. Revenue decisions must be evidence-backed, owner-assigned, and time-bounded.
2. No commercial commitment may be executed without documented downside analysis.
3. Every strategic offer must define margin floor, retention objective, and failure trigger.
4. Commercial approvals must be reproducible from repository evidence only.
5. Operational speed is permitted only where rollback cost is bounded and visible.
6. Exception paths must be explicit, scarce, and reviewable.
7. Customer acquisition, expansion, and retention policies must be measured independently.
8. Incentives must reward durable value creation, not cosmetic growth.
9. Commercial authority must map to written ownership, escalation, and override limits.
10. Forecasting must separate committed revenue, probable revenue, and speculative pipeline.

## Non-Negotiable Controls
- Define approval thresholds for discounting, concessions, and non-standard terms.
- Require written commercial rationale for every material pricing deviation.
- Maintain a decision trail for high-value opportunities and losses.
- Track gross margin integrity at offer, close, and renewal stages.
- Reject undefined service expansion that creates hidden delivery liabilities.
- Encode retention protections for strategically material accounts.
- Record escalation criteria for legal, delivery, finance, and governance review.
- Prevent dependency on undocumented verbal commitments.
- Require owner confirmation before any externally binding promise.
- Treat commercial ambiguity as unresolved risk, not momentum.

## Decision Gates
### Gate 1: Strategic Fit
- Confirm account fit, buying authority, and delivery feasibility.
- Reject pursuit where fit is weak and rescue economics are unclear.

### Gate 2: Economic Integrity
- Confirm price logic, margin preservation, and downside containment.
- Reject deals that normalize loss-making behavior without explicit authorization.

### Gate 3: Execution Readiness
- Confirm staffing, capability, operational capacity, and escalation path.
- Reject promises that rely on heroic execution without structural support.

### Gate 4: Retention Defensibility
- Confirm renewal path, expansion logic, and relationship durability.
- Reject wins that structurally increase churn or support burden.

## Operating Requirements
- All material commercial doctrine must be repository-native.
- Governance-linked strategic commercial documents must be indexed and manifest-listed.
- Contract-style validation must verify document presence and references.
- Changes must pass governance hardening, constitution validation, and test execution before merge.

## Failure Conditions
The commercial posture is non-compliant if any of the following occurs:
- pricing authority is unclear,
- discount approval is undocumented,
- margin floor is absent,
- strategic commitment lacks an accountable owner,
- repository references are broken,
- validation coverage is missing,
- external promise exceeds documented operating capacity.

## Enforcement Posture
No top-level commercial hardening change is complete until:
1. the doctrine document exists in-repo,
2. repository references are added,
3. validation tests pass,
4. pull request checks pass,
5. the change is merged into main.

## Status
Approved for repository integration upon successful validation.
