# Monetization and Market Doctrine

This document defines the canonical monetization and market doctrine for the ASO-X repository.

## Commercial Mandate

The project is intended to support commercially valuable, durable, and globally competitive outcomes.

## Strategic Commercial Principles

- Prioritize capabilities that create monetization capacity.
- Prefer changes that reduce operating cost, reduce risk, increase automation, or strengthen defensibility.
- Maintain suitability for private operators, enterprise customers, and commercial deployments.
- Preserve extensibility for downstream derivative offerings and tailored implementations.
- Treat reliability, governance, retention, and intelligence value as commercial assets.

## Non-Decorative Rule

Non-commercial, decorative, vanity-only, or strategically weak changes are subordinate to durability, monetization, reliability, and strategic value.

## Repository Requirement

Commercial intent must be reflected in repository-native doctrine, roadmap, interfaces, and validation-backed priorities rather than depending on memory-only context.
