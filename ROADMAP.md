# ASO-X 50-Year Roadmap

## Mission

Build durable global financial intelligence infrastructure with strong governance, auditability, and revenue orientation.

## Horizon 1: Foundation

- Preserve canonical governance.
- Keep repo-native state current.
- Validate all changes locally and in CI.

## Horizon 2: Revenue Readiness

- Convert intelligence capabilities into measurable value.
- Keep high-risk financial actions manually gated.
- Maintain audit trails for decisions and artifacts.

## Horizon 3: Durable Automation

- Expand safe autopilot functions.
- Keep policy matrix as the boundary for automation.
- Use PRs and tests as the default governance enforcement path.

## Horizon 4: 50-Year Durability

- Prefer simple, documented, portable systems.
- Preserve artifact history.
- Avoid hidden operational state outside the repo.
