# ASO-X Canonical Roadmap

## Vision: Formal-Grade Governance
The ASO-X project is committed to evolving from evidence-based assurance to **mathematically proven correctness**. Every governance-critical component must eventually reach a state of formal verification.

## Strategic Objectives
1.  **Policy Triad Completion (IMMEDIATE):** Define and deploy missing retention, safety, and evidence policies.
2.  **Hash-Chain Evidence Ledger (NEAR-TERM):** Transition from independent hashes to immutable linked-chains.
3.  **Formal Specification (MEDIUM-TERM):** Develop TLA+/Formal specs for Roadmap Authority and Enforcement logic.
4.  **Mathematical Proof of Correctness (LONG-TERM):** Integrate automated formal verification into the release gate.
