# Enterprise Acceptance Criteria

An ASO-X enterprise capability is acceptable only if it satisfies at least one of these outcomes:

- Protects real money.
- Unlocks a blocked financial or governance decision.
- Proves control over a material process.
- Improves auditability of a high-stakes artifact.
- Reduces the probability or cost of expensive human error.

## Non-Negotiable Boundary

If a capability cannot be connected to real-money control, proof, enforcement, audit, or decision acceleration, it is not enterprise-core.
