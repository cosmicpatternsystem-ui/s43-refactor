# ASO-X Business Positioning

## Top-Level Decision

ASO-X will not compete as a generic developer tool.

ASO-X will become the enterprise-grade governance and financial intelligence control layer for real-money software operations.

## Business Lock

LOCK-BIZ-001: ASO-X must protect money, unlock money, or prove control over money.

Any feature, artifact, workflow, or automation that does not support risk reduction, capital protection, auditability, revenue enablement, or enforceable decision control is secondary.

## Target Buyer

ASO-X is built for organizations where software decisions can create material financial, operational, audit, or compliance risk.

Primary buyers:

- Financial technology companies
- Funds and trading operations
- Compliance-driven software organizations
- Platform engineering teams
- Risk and audit teams
- Enterprise release governance teams
- Organizations operating real-money systems

## Pain With Money Attached

The target customer pays because uncontrolled software operations create expensive failure modes:

- Unclear source of truth
- Unprovable decisions
- Missing evidence
- Weak release gates
- Manual audit preparation
- Policy drift across repositories
- High-risk changes without enforceable controls
- Lost institutional memory
- Slow or unsafe operational decisions

## Enterprise Value Proposition

ASO-X makes real-money software operations auditable, enforceable, and durable.

It provides:

- Repository-backed source of truth
- Immutable decision and artifact retention
- Governance locks
- Policy matrix enforcement
- Release and evidence gates
- Local and CI validation
- Durable operational memory
- Readiness signals for real-money operations

## Product Boundary

ASO-X is not a generic productivity tool.

ASO-X must remain focused on:

- Financial intelligence
- Governance enforcement
- Audit evidence
- Artifact retention
- Durable decision systems
- Real-money operational resilience
- Enterprise-grade control planes

## Pricing Hypothesis

The commercial model should follow an open-core strategy.

Open layer:

- CLI validation
- Canonical schemas
- Governance baseline
- Local checks
- Basic CI gates
- Documentation artifacts

Enterprise layer:

- Hosted evidence registry
- Multi-repository governance
- Organization policy enforcement
- Audit export
- Risk intelligence
- SSO and RBAC
- Approval workflows
- Compliance reporting
- Executive readiness dashboards

Potential annual contract value:

- Small regulated teams: USD 25k to 50k
- Mid-market enterprise teams: USD 50k to 150k
- Large financial or compliance-heavy organizations: USD 150k to 250k+

## North Star Metric

Number of real-money decisions made auditable, enforceable, and durable.

## Non-Negotiable Rule

If ASO-X cannot protect money, unlock money, or prove control over money, it is not a global-grade business.
