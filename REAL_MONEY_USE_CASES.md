# Real-Money Use Cases

## Use Cases

- Prove that a high-value financial decision followed approved governance.
- Detect policy drift before it creates operational or regulatory loss.
- Preserve immutable evidence for audits and internal reviews.
- Enforce decision boundaries before capital is exposed.
- Convert governance artifacts into executable enterprise controls.

## Exclusion

ASO-X must not position itself as a generic productivity tool when the use case has no connection to capital protection, risk control, auditability, or financial decision speed.
