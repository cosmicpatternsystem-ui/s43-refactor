# Buyer Personas

## Primary Buyers

- Chief Risk Officer
- Chief Compliance Officer
- Chief Financial Officer
- Head of Internal Audit
- Enterprise Governance Lead
- Fintech Operations Lead
- Investment Operations Lead

## Buyer Trigger

The buyer acts when uncontrolled decisions, missing evidence, weak auditability, or slow financial governance can create material loss.

## Buying Logic

ASO-X is valuable when the buyer needs stronger control over real-money decisions, not when the buyer only wants developer tooling.
