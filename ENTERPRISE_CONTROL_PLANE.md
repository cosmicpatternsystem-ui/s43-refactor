# Enterprise Control Plane

ASO-X is positioned as a Financial Intelligence and Governance Control Plane for real-money systems.

## Control Plane Purpose

ASO-X must help enterprise teams control, verify, enforce, and audit decisions where money, risk, or regulatory exposure is involved.

## Core Outcomes

- Protect capital from preventable operational failure.
- Unlock faster financial and risk decisions with evidence.
- Prove control over governance, policy, and execution artifacts.
- Reduce expensive ambiguity in enterprise decision systems.
- Preserve durable audit trails for high-stakes environments.

## LOCK-BIZ-002

Every enterprise-facing ASO-X capability must map to a real-money control, proof, or enforcement outcome.
